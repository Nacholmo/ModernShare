# Contributing

Use [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) for build, analysis and tests.
Explain the affected camera path, expected behavior and validation. Include the
stock/build hashes and clearly separate emulator results from camera results.

Keep changes within audited allocations and preserve original feature/recovery
images locally. Never submit firmware binaries, camera photos, memory dumps,
vendor decompilation exports, installed toolchains or credentials.

Contributions are offered under AGPL-3.0-or-later unless they are explicitly
identified compatible third-party material with its original licence preserved.
Retain copyright and attribution notices. The repository's licences do not grant
rights to third-party firmware, trademarks, logos or characters.
